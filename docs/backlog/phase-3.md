# Phase 3 backlog — Client portal, WhatsApp and going live

**Goal:** an agency runs its live clients on the app: clients pick topics, approve scripts and review videos in their own portal, messages reach them on WhatsApp, posts and reports reach them, and invoices can be paid online.
**Exit gate (P3-14):** a client opens their portal, picks topics and approves a script; gets a WhatsApp approval request for a video and asks for changes, which appear on the video; the month's report is released and the client is told on WhatsApp; an invoice is paid through a payment link and marked paid by itself; a person in another agency sees none of it.

**Self-service, like everything else.** Each agency connects its own WhatsApp number, its clients' social accounts and its own Razorpay account from the app, with step-by-step help. Platform accounts that are ours (our Meta and Google apps for one-click connections, hosting) are set up in the last step; until then the manual way keeps working (manual posting with proof, entering WhatsApp details by hand).

Decisions (2026-10-02): clients sign in to the portal with a **private link per contact** (like the onboarding link, replaceable and switched off by the agency); each agency **enters its own WhatsApp Cloud API details** (phone number ID and access token); one-click Meta sign-up may come later.

## Progress

**P3-01 to P3-05 · The client portal** — **Done.** On the client's page, Client portal makes a private link for each contact (only its hash is kept; a new link stops the old one; switched off at once), shown once with Copy and a ready WhatsApp message. The portal (`/app/c/<link>`, phone-first, in the agency's name, logo and colour) shows what waits for the client, and lets them pick the month's topics and say they are done (the team is told), approve a script or ask for changes with a note, watch a video version and comment at a moment in it ("0:18"), approve it or ask for changes (the team classifies them as before), see and print their issued invoices, and ask the team something — answered from Client requests. Everything is limited to the contact's client, goes through the same rules as the team's screens, and is recorded in the audit log as done by that contact. `/portal/:link/…`, `/clients/:id/portal-links`, `/client-requests`.

**P3-06 · Encrypted secrets** — **Done.** AES-256-GCM with `SECRETS_KEY`; tokens are never shown back (only their last characters) nor written to the audit log.

**P3-07 and P3-08 · WhatsApp** — **Done** with each agency's own WhatsApp Cloud API number (Settings → WhatsApp): the phone number ID and permanent token (checked against WhatsApp at once, with the reason when refused), the app secret, quiet hours (default 9 pm to 8 am India time), the callback URL and verify token to paste into Meta, the templates it had approved — the app suggests the wording for each — and a test message. Messages go only to a client's approvers who agreed (recorded by the team with how, or chosen by the contact in their portal; STOP turns them off), each with their own portal link, as background jobs with retries; every message is in WhatsApp messages (and on the client's page), a skipped one with the reason. Sent by themselves: approval requests for scripts and video versions with Approve and Request changes buttons, onboarding reminders on their day (the team is asked only when WhatsApp cannot take it), a video gone live, an invoice ready. What comes back is believed only when Meta signs it: receipts update the log (never stepping back), Approve or Request changes acts on the script or video as the client (in the audit log as them), the words that follow a change request become their comment, and anything else reaches whoever looks after the client. `/whatsapp`, `/webhooks/whatsapp/:id`, `/clients/:id/contacts/:id/whatsapp`.

**P3-09 · Monthly report** — **Done.** Monthly reports: for each client with a running agreement, the month's videos promised and delivered, each published post with its link and numbers (entered by the team for now — the platform connections bring them in later), the team's note and next month's picked topics, as a printable document in the agency's name and colour. Drafts are made by themselves on the 25th (whoever looks after the client is told) or by the team any time, refreshed while a draft, then released: it shows in the client's portal under Reports, the approvers hear on WhatsApp, and it stays exactly as released. `/reports`, `/publishing/posts/:id/metrics`, `/portal/:link/reports`.

**P3-10 · Payments** — **Done** with each agency's own Razorpay account (Settings → Payments): its key ID and secret (checked at once; test or live shown), and the webhook URL and secret to paste into Razorpay. Every issued invoice gets a payment link by itself (a background job, so issuing never waits on Razorpay) — on the invoice page with Copy, and as Pay now in the client's portal; a link Razorpay refused, or one for an invoice issued before payments were connected, is made from the invoice page. When Razorpay says a link was paid (believed only with its signature), the payment is recorded once however often the notice comes, the invoice is marked paid with the method and reference, and finance is told; a part payment is recorded without marking it paid. A cancelled invoice's link is switched off. `/payments`, `/invoices/:id/pay-link`, `/webhooks/razorpay/:id`.

**P3-13 · Calendar: follow-ups** — **Done.** People who see sales find each open lead's next follow-up on the calendar (late ones marked), and a role limited to its own leads sees only its own; a follow-up opens that lead on the Sales page. Leads that are won or lost drop off.
